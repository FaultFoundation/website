Privacy is a sensitive topic. We’ve heard concerns from some members about sharing personal information through our verification process, and we want to address those concerns directly and honestly. **We’re trying to work with and provide a service to _people_, not _users_.** That distinction matters to us. Users are metrics on a dashboard. People are individuals with concerns, boundaries, and trust that must be earned.

- We encourage you to reach out Privacy Policy in full here: [https://fault.foundation/privacy-policy/](/privacy-policy/)
- Here’s a wonderful video to practice reading Privacy Policies: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQqR4tKRiKs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQqR4tKRiKs)

## Our Mission and Values

**The Fault Foundation was established** for _Gamers Supporting Students_**.** Every program, resource, and opportunity we offer has been designed with students in mind. To ensure our services reach the individuals they were created for, we must verify the student status of each member of the Discord community. This is accomplished through collection of your Discord account ID and school-issued email address.

## What We Collect and How We Protect It

We practice **data minimization**, meaning we only collect what’s necessary to verify eligibility and facilitate community interaction. Our registration process asks for your Discord username and account ID to link your verification to your Discord account, your school name and school-issued email address to confirm your student or alumni status, your age range to understand our community demographics, and brief background information only for manual verification when automated methods aren’t possible. **That’s it**.

We do not sell your data, we do not trade it, we do not rent it, and we do not distribute it to third parties. Your information is used exclusively for verification and moderation purposes.

Access to registration data is **restricted to the Board of Directors**. General members and volunteer moderators do not have access to raw registration data. All Board members are required to have two-factor authentication enabled, and we regularly review audit logs and security reports.

## Your Rights

You have control over your data. You can request access to see what information we hold about you, request corrections if anything is outdated or incorrect, and request deletion of your registration data entirely. To exercise any of these rights, open a support ticket or contact a Board member directly.

## Discord’s Inherent Lack of Privacy

By using Discord at all, you’ve already agreed to share significant personal information with a third party. Discord collects a wide range of data that you actively provide, including your username, password, email address, phone number, date of birth, and any content you create such as messages, posts, and profile information. If you make purchases through Discord, they also collect your credit card details, billing addresses, and transaction history.

Beyond what you submit directly, Discord gathers information from third-party accounts you choose to link (such as Twitch, Spotify, or gaming platforms) and can access your contact list if you enable contact syncing. Additionally, Discord receives information from external sources, including data shared by other users about you, information from advertisers and marketing partners, and data from analytics services, all of which may be combined with information Discord already holds about you.

Regarding retention, Discord keeps your personal information for as long as they determine it’s needed for the purposes it was collected, which typically means as long as your account remains active. Even after you delete your account, some data may be retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or to resolve disputes.

Our verification process asks for _additional_ information, handled within our community under a clear, published policy with defined access controls and user rights. The Fault Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit bound by legal compliance requirements under the Missouri Nonprofit Corporation Act and IRS regulations, not a corporation monetizing your attention.

## The Truth

Not everyone will be comfortable with our verification process, and there’s not much we can do to change that. We understand. Privacy is personal, and everyone’s threshold for sharing information is different. If our verification process doesn’t align with your comfort level, we respect that decision. If you have specific concerns about our privacy practices, open a support ticket or reach out to a Board member. We’re happy to explain our processes in detail.
