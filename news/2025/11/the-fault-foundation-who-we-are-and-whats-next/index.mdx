We believe the best way to start is with a proper introduction. In this post, we’ll cover who we are, the dedicated team behind the scenes, and the exciting work we have planned for the near future.

## The Fault Foundation

The Fault Foundation was established in July 2025 by five founding members who wanted to create a community of gamers supporting students.

> “The Fault Foundation advances gaming culture and education by creating inclusive competitive gaming opportunities; fostering community connections between gamers across diverse backgrounds; and developing educational programs that promote teamwork, strategic thinking, and digital citizenship.”

These members initially met after President Oscar Labit coordinated two Overwatch teams for an upcoming tournament. In September 2025, Fault Foundation Inc. became a registered 501(c)(3) organization, providing access to tools and partnership opportunities across the nation. You can learn more by reading the bylaws [here](/bylaws/).

## Introductions

Five Board Members and three Officers organize, review, and execute projects relating to the Fault Foundation’s mission. They are from universities across the Midwest, including Michigan State University, University of Southern Indiana, and Schoolcraft College, and major in subjects across business and liberal arts. You can learn more about our founders [here](/about/).

> ### Oscar Labit
>
> “I’m a senior at Michigan State University with a major in accounting and a minor in Japanese. I founded the Fault Foundation because I believe education is for everyone, and I want to use my hobby of playing video games to provide professional and academic services to students across the world.”
>
> ### Wesley Shaver
>
> “I’m a junior at the University of Southern Indiana, where I’m pursuing a degree in accounting and professional services. I chose to be part of the Fault Foundation because I’m interested in providing opportunities for students to have community-focused environments relevant to their interests. I hope to connect students and provide them with a network where they can find like-minded peers and support for their academic goals.”
>
> ### Ryan Schmidt
>
> “I’m a senior at Michigan State University, where I study supply chain management with a minor in International Business. I decided to be part of the Fault Foundation because I love video games and want to support students who share the same passion by creating a community where we can grow professionally and personally.”

## Our Future

The Fault Foundation officers are working on several exciting projects. You can read more about our roadmap [here](/roadmap/) (https://fault.foundation/roadmap/).

If you’re interested in helping develop any of these programs, please reach out to Oscar Labit via email at [oscar.labit@fault.foundation](mailto:oscar.labit@fault.foundation). We look forward to building a community of like-minded individuals while providing academic and professional support to students everywhere.
