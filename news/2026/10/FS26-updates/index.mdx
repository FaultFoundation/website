Hello everyone! I'm sorry for the lack of communication recently. We've been doing a ton of work since the conclusion of our first tournament to ensure future tournaments provide the premium, beneficial experience for everyone we wanted when we started. This has involved revamping the rulebook with clearer language and examples, securing coaching, providing trophies, and lots more. The work we've done promotes a sustainable and scalable future for our organization and helps ensure consistent operations. We're super excited for our next tournament to start in January 2027.

## The Esports Commons

One issue we ran into when hosting Overfault: Season 0 was operating with limited volunteers. Over the last 3 months, we've been developing, testing, and implementing better technologies to help provide a quality experience for our players and promote expansion without substantially increasing our staff size. Through this process, we developed a number of open-source tools and published the Esports Commons. A list of features is provided below:

- Hosting internal Overfault tournaments to reduce barriers to entry and ensure a high-quality experience for both players and staff.
- Aggregating tournament information, results, and statistics from across platforms, such as FACEIT, start.gg, Challonge, LeagueOS, and Discord, to help teams easily discover tournaments to compete in.
- Organizing player, team, and match statistics from across games, tournaments, and more in one portal to provide useful analyses for team managers and coaches.
- Providing FACEIT team scouting information to lower the barrier to entry for new teams competing with limited resources.

Everything is open-source! If you don't want to use our platform, feel free to host it yourself. Our goal is to provide a service to the community. We've also open-sourced our main website and Overwatch data collection system. We're working to release the Discord bot and all other existing projects by the end of the year: [github.com/FaultFoundation](https://github.com/FaultFoundation/)

Our upcoming projects for the site include:

- Better LAN/in-person tournament filtering and information.
- Skill ratings headlining tournaments.
- Player statistics, scouting, and series/league features are currently experimental. We're polishing them up for a full release by the end of the year.

## Career Centers for Collegiate Esports

As part of our roadmap, we're building a career center for students involved in collegiate esports. Existing career centers put universities at the center, but this often limits students' access to a network outside certain cities and states. By using games as the common connection between people, we can promote connections across universities without replacing existing resources. If you're interested in learning more or getting involved, feel free to send me a DM!

We're actively applying for grants to fund the program with an estimated launch date of fall 2027.
