Hello everyone! We’re glad you’re interested in the Fault Foundation Discord community. This short post explains more information regarding the verification process.

## Protection

To keep our community safe and minimize spam or abuse, you’ll notice new accounts have limited access to the channels within the “Public” category. We’ve decided to add this barrier to protect our community because people who may share personal information relating to their education and career.

- To get the “Member” role and gain access to all our discussions and features, please complete the [Member Registration Form](https://forms.gle/hhthzetWHCHpUgdW7) located in your bot DMs. If you didn’t receive a DM, please contact a Support member.
- If you have any questions, comments, or concerns, feel free to send a message in #⁠new-chat.

## Verification

The verification workflow (less than 5 minutes):

- You should receive a DM from the Fault Foundation bot with an invite to complete the member registration form.
- Upon completion, you should receive an email with a verification code.
- Once verified, the bot with automatically give you access to the rest of the server.
- If there’s an issue with your form, a support ticket will automatically be opened. These are typically resolved within 24 hours.

## Information Requested

Here’s a list of all information requested in the verification form:

- Student status
- Discord username and ID
- Age range (for legal compliance)
- University/high school name
- University/high school website
- University-/high school-issued email address
- University/high school estimated graduation date

## Privacy

The Fault Foundation does not sell, trade, rent, or distribute personal data to third parties. All information collected is used exclusively for verifying student or alumni status and for server moderation purposes. Access to personal data is strictly limited to the Board of Directors. This policy ensures transparency and accountability in how member information is handled.
